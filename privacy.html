Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers the website at omega4.tech. It is deliberately short because the site collects very little.

1. Information we collect

This website does not use accounts, contact forms, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics scripts. We do not set cookies.

Standard server logs — IP address, request path, user agent, timestamp — are generated by our hosting provider for security and operational purposes and retained according to their policy.

2. Email

If you write to an address at this domain, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to keep a record of the enquiry. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to a marketing list.

3. Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except where required by law, or where a service provider processes it on our behalf under their own safeguards.

4. Retention

Correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and any resulting engagement, then removed.

5. Your rights

You may ask what personal data we hold about you, request correction, or request deletion by writing to hello@omega4.tech.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date above changes with it.
